Active prevention hardware (harmonics, low frequency, voltage) & Damaging appliances or changing supply frequency raises safety and legal objections; no field evidence; not recommended. Smart prepaid metering & Already at scale in BPDB and DPDC; in Nigeria each 1\% increase in metered customers was associated with 0.8\% lower ATC\&C loss; bypassing upstream of the meter remains. C8: Uses existing utility data or routine utility works; lawful and safe under present rules. Score 3. & Effect on theft in Bangladesh not measured; evidence from one regulatory panel (Nigeria). & Affordability and acceptance concerns for low-income households. & \citealp{odje2021aggregated} \\

Score 1. & No field evaluation. & Offers a legal route and protects livelihoods if introduced before enforcement. & \citealp{awal2019bangladesh,sakib2024banglateslas} \\
